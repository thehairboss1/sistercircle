Safe House/ Shelter

A Safe Place to Begin Again

The Sister Circle’s Safe House provides a secure, supportive environment for women and their children who need a safe place away from domestic violence. Our goal is to offer more than a roof—we provide a space where survivors can breathe, feel protected, and begin taking the steps toward a new and independent life. Residents can receive support with safety planning, basic needs, emotional wellness, and connections to trusted community resources.

You Are Safe Here.

We believe leaving an abusive situation is a courageous first step, and no woman should have to face that journey alone. Our team works with each survivor to help create a personalized path forward, connecting her with resources for housing, legal assistance, counseling, employment, financial stability, and other essential services. For the safety and privacy of our residents, the location of our Safe House is confidential. What matters most is that every woman who comes through our doors knows she is welcomed, valued, and deserving of a life filled with safety, peace, and freedom.

Counseling

We believe you are your own life expert. All you need is a safe and supportive space where you can reclaim the freedom to make your own choices, utilize your strengths and abilities, and actively develop and accomplish your goals. We offer individual services and have weekly group meetings with community members who have experienced domestic and intimate partner abuse. In addition, we also provide individual services to supportive family and friends. Our supportive counseling services are provided at different locations throughout the LehighValley. These services are in a safe and confidential space where support, information, and advocacy can be found at no cost. For more information and to connect with our supportive counseling services, please call our 24/7 Helpline at 1800-888-8888.

YOUTH SERVICES Helping kids learn about healthy relationships

We provide supportive, confidential services for children and teens who have experienced or been affected by domestic and intimate partner violence. Through individual counseling, school-based advocacy, and group programs like I AM HER and SPACES, we help young people explore healthy relationships, build emotional resilience, and feel empowered in their own voices. Our team works closely with schools, families, and community partners to ensure every child and teen has access to a safe, supportive space where they can heal, grow, and thrive.
